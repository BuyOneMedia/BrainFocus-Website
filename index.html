How Target Cages Work

Calcudoku combines the single-digit placement of Sudoku with elementary mathematical operations. Numbers in an outlined cage must combine to produce the top-left clue.

+

Addition Cages (e.g. 7+)

Sum of all cells in this cage must equal 7 (e.g. 4 + 3 or 1 + 2 + 4).

×

Multiplication (e.g. 12×)

Product of all cage cells equals 12 (e.g. 3 × 4 or 2 × 2 × 3).

−

Subtraction (e.g. 2−)

Difference between 2 cells equals 2 in any order (e.g. 4 − 2 = 2 or 3 − 1 = 2).

÷

Division (e.g. 2÷)

Larger cell divided by smaller cell equals 2 (e.g. 4 ÷ 2 = 2 or 2 ÷ 1 = 2).

💡

Pro Tip: Numbers can repeat within the same cage if they are in different rows and columns (e.g. in an L-shaped cage), but never within the same row or column!
