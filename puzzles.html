Try a Quick 4×4 Calcudoku Puzzle

Fill each row and column with the numbers 1, 2, 3, and 4 so no number repeats. Each outlined group of boxes ("cage") must combine to make the small clue number in its corner.

Click a box and enter a number from 1 to 4.

How to Play

Like Sudoku, every row and every column uses each number from 1 to 4 once. The thick teal lines group boxes into "cages" with a small math clue in the top-left corner:

  • + Addition (e.g. 7+): The numbers inside the cage add up to 7 (such as 3 + 4).
  • − Subtraction (e.g. 1−): The two numbers have a difference of 1 in any order (such as 4 and 3).
  • × Multiplication (e.g. 8×): The numbers multiply together to make 8 (such as 2 × 4).
  • ÷ Division (e.g. 2÷): Dividing the larger number by the smaller number gives 2 (such as 4 ÷ 2).
